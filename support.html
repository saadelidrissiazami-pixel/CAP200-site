Capose Support

Last updated: November 24, 2026

How can we help?

For a purchase, subscription, privacy or technical question, email azappmi@gmail.com. Include the device model, iOS version and a short description of the issue. Never send a password or full payment information.

Subscriptions

Capose Plus is a monthly or yearly subscription that opens all 200 steps while active. A first subscription starts with a 3-day free trial: cancel at least 24 hours before it ends in your Apple account settings and you are not charged. Purchases and cancellations are managed by Apple. Use “Restore Purchases” in Capose if an active subscription is not recognized.

Safety

Choose busy public places during the day for outdoor steps. Respect other people’s boundaries, keep personal details private and ask a trusted adult for support when needed. In an emergency, contact local emergency services.
