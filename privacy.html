PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

Effective: November 24, 2026

Capose (formerly CAP200) requires an account, created with Sign in with Apple or an e-mail address, to access the steps. The app is for people aged 15 or older. Journal reflections always stay on the device.

Information processed

Capose processes challenge progress, favorites, optional journal text, feelings, language preference and the answers to the welcome questionnaire (such as your goal, pace, preferred time for reminders and age range: 15, 16, 17, or 18 or older) on the device. The answers and results of the personality test also stay on the device. Only the goal and pace are included in the account backup; the other answers, including the age range, stay on the device. The publisher does not receive the content of journal reflections or feelings through the app.

For subscriptions, Apple processes payment information and RevenueCat provides purchase status to Capose. Capose receives subscription status and technical purchase identifiers needed to unlock paid access. RevenueCat receives a random account number, never the e-mail address. The publisher does not receive full card details.

Account and progress backup

When you create an account, Capose stores on servers hosted by Cloudflare in the European Union: your e-mail address, a random account number, the dates of creation and last activity, your marketing choice and its date, and a backup of your progress (started and completed steps and their dates, favorites, opened domains, goal and pace). The backup does not include journal text or feelings.

This data is used to sign you in, restore your progress on another phone and contact you about your account. Sign-in codes are sent by Resend, which receives your e-mail address for that purpose, with e-mails sent from the European Union. Codes are stored in protected form and expire after 10 minutes. To limit abuse, a protected form of the e-mail address and IP address is kept for at most 24 hours.

With Sign in with Apple, Apple sends Capose a signed token with the e-mail address you choose to share, which can be a private relay address. Capose checks the token with Apple’s public keys and uses that address like an e-mail account. Capose does not receive your Apple password.

Marketing e-mails are optional and off by default. You can change this choice at any time in the Me tab.

Anonymous ranking

If “Join the anonymous ranking” is on (it can be turned off in the Me tab), the app sends a random number created on the device, the current week and the number of each completed challenge to a server hosted by Cloudflare. This data is not linked to your account. No name, e-mail address, feeling, note, goal or device identifier is sent, and IP addresses are not stored. The app only receives totals (how many people completed a challenge, your share of the most active this week). Raw records are deleted after 8 weeks; only per-challenge totals are kept. Turning the option off deletes the random number from the device.

Advertising measurement

Capose includes the Meta (Facebook) SDK to measure whether its ads on Facebook and Instagram lead to app installs and use of the app. The SDK sends Meta technical events (app install or launch, onboarding completed, account created, number of a completed challenge, island unlocked, in-app subscription purchase with its amount and currency) with basic device and app information (device model, iOS version, app version, language) and the IP address seen by Meta. Capose does not ask for tracking permission, does not collect the advertising identifier (IDFA) and does not send journal content, feelings, questionnaire answers, names or e-mail addresses to Meta. Meta uses this data as described in its privacy policy, including Aggregated Event Measurement and Apple SKAdNetwork.

Purpose and retention

Local data is used to show progress and unlock steps. It remains on the device until the app data is deleted or the app is removed. Deleting your account removes your account and its backup from the servers straight away, and also erases your challenges, notes and goal from the phone. An account inactive for about 2 years receives a warning e-mail, then is deleted 30 days later if you do not sign in. Subscription records are retained by Apple and RevenueCat under their respective policies and legal obligations.

Sharing

Data is shared only with the service providers needed to run Capose: Apple and RevenueCat for subscriptions, Cloudflare for hosting, and Resend for e-mail. Advertising measurement events are shared with Meta as described above. Capose does not sell personal information or use journal content for advertising.

Children and teenagers

Capose is for people aged 15 or older: by continuing on the sign-in screen, you confirm you are at least 15. People under 15 should not use the app. The welcome questionnaire asks for your age range to get to know you; this answer stays on the device and is not sent to the publisher, Meta or any other service. Capose does not request a name, date of birth, precise location, contacts or camera access. A parent or guardian should review purchase settings for a minor.

Your choices

You can sign out or delete your account in the Me tab of the app. Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your data, to object to its use, and to complain to your data protection authority (the CNIL in France). You can delete local Capose data by removing the app. Subscription management and cancellation are available in Apple account settings. For a privacy request, contact azappmi@gmail.com.

Changes

This policy may be updated when the app or its service providers change. The effective date above will be updated.

Resend envoie les codes de connexion, valables 10 minutes. Les e-mails marketing sont facultatifs et désactivés par défaut. Tu peux te déconnecter, changer ce choix ou supprimer ton compte dans l’onglet Moi : la suppression efface immédiatement le compte et sa sauvegarde des serveurs, ainsi que tes défis, tes notes et ton objectif sur le téléphone. Apple traite le paiement et RevenueCat transmet le statut de l’abonnement, avec un numéro aléatoire et jamais ton e-mail. Si le classement anonyme est activé (désactivable dans l’onglet Moi), l’app envoie un numéro aléatoire, la semaine et le numéro de chaque défi réalisé, sans lien avec ton compte ; ces données brutes sont supprimées après 8 semaines. L’éditeur ne reçoit pas les coordonnées bancaires complètes et ne vend pas de données personnelles. Selon ton pays, tu peux demander l’accès à tes données, leur correction, leur suppression ou leur copie, t’opposer à leur usage et saisir ton autorité de protection des données (la CNIL en France).
